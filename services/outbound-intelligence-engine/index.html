The math behind outbound.

Every growth channel has a cost-per-conversation. Inbound compounds slowly. Paid media scales expensively. Events are one-off. Referrals are unpredictable.

Outbound is different. When done with precision, it is the only channel where you choose exactly who enters your pipeline, and every cost is tied directly to a conversation with a qualified buyer.

Average B2B cost per qualified lead through paid channels: Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 15,000+. Most go cold within a week.

Outbound identifies the specific company, the specific person, and the specific reason to reach out. Every touch is intentional.

When a single new account can change a quarter's trajectory, the economics of precision outbound are hard to argue with.

How it

Works.

We don't do batch-and-blast. The Engine is a precision-engineered outbound system designed to identify, verify, and engage your ideal buyers systematically.

01

ICP Diagnostic

We start by defining your Ideal Customer Profile with precision.

Who exactly are you trying to reach? What titles? What company size? What geography? What signals indicate they are ready? If outbound is not the right channel for your business, we will tell you in this conversation itself.

02
02

Engine Configuration

We build your targeting criteria, set up verification workflows, configure your CRM pipeline, and draft your outreach sequences.

Every word, every targeting filter, and every workflow is reviewed with you before anything goes live.

03
03

Discovery and Enrichment

We run structured searches across live market data to find companies and people matching your ICP.

Each prospect is individually enriched with recent company news, leadership changes, and trigger signals. No batch-and-blast. Each record is built with intent.

04
04

Outreach Deployment

Personalised, multi-step sequences go live across email and LinkedIn. Each message is tailored to the individual.

Each follow-up adds value instead of repeating the ask. The cadence, tone, and channel mix are calibrated to your buyer's world.

05
05

Weekly Intelligence Cycles

Every week, we review what worked, what did not, and what the data is telling us.

Messaging is refined. Targeting is adjusted. New prospects are added. The engine improves with every cycle, not every quarter.

06
06

Pipeline Handoff

When a prospect responds with interest, we handle the initial reply, qualify intent, and prepare the handoff to your sales team with full context.

By the time your closer picks up the conversation, they know exactly who they are speaking to and why.

Precision is the product.

Most outbound providers sell volume. We sell accuracy.

Every prospect in your pipeline has been individually discovered through live market data, not scraped from a purchased database. Every email address has been verified before it is ever touched. Every opening line has been written around a real event in that company's world. Every sequence has been structured to respect the buyer's time and intelligence.

The difference is felt in the numbers that matter: open rates, reply rates, and the quality of conversations that reach your sales team. When the foundation is precise, the outcomes follow.

The engine uses AI for enrichment, signal detection, and personalisation at scale. But the strategy, the ICP, and the targeting logic are human-built. Automation without direction is noise. We handle both.
